## What is embedding?
 An embeddings converts each word into a list of numbers called vectors that captures its meaning.
## Why not use keyword search?
Keyword search will require the word to be present in the document if the model want to reply whereas in the embeddings it understands words that are similar instead of exact words
## What is cosine Similarity?
Cosine similarity measures angle between two vectors and measures how closely two embeddings point in the same direction.

same direction -> similar
othogonal or 90 degree -> then not related
opposite directions -> then completely opposite
