## What is a retriever?
A Retriever is a component that finds the most relevant chunks of information for a user's query
## How is a retriever different from a vector database?
Vector databases store embeddings and performs similarity search whereas
retriever uses the vector db to fetch relevamt documents. 
## What does k=3 mean?
Means to return top 3 most relevant chunks.

## Why do we retrieve multiple chunks?
Because important information is often spread across several chunks.
