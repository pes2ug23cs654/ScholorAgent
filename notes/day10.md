## What is a retriever?
A Retriever is a component that searches our knowledge base and returns the most relevant documents/chunks for a query.
## What does retriever.invoke() return?
returns relevant chunks 
## Why is retrieval important?
Without retrieval LLM answers only from what it learned during training 
## What happens if retrieval is poor?
If retireval is wrong it can lead to wrong answer and hallucination and incomplete answer.
